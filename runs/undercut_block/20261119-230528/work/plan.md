# Plan undercut_block (AL6061, stock 84x54x32, vacuum, Z0 = bottom)
T-slot head (Z15-21, |y|<10) is undercut except where the drafted pocket opens into it (X10.787-33.213, Y+-6.212 at Z21).
Neck floor is at Z21.01 (0.01 skin over the head) - finish it exactly, never go below it outside the pocket opening.
1. T1 D10 18000rpm: face Z31/Z30 (ae 6); outer profile rough offset 5.25, ap 8 (ae ~1.75);
   Y-slot (X-36..-20, Z22) rough ap 2 (centre full-width then 2 side passes, 0.25 left);
   neck (Y+-6, Z21.01) rough ap ~2.25; pocket opening below Z21 rough by zig-zag ramp to Z15.25 + contour;
   finish slot floor/walls Z22, neck floor/walls Z21.01, opening floor Z15; profile finish (upper pass tip Z5 at
   offset 5.00, lower pass tip Z-0.3 at offset 5.01 so the shank above 25 mm flute never rubs).
2. T10 drill 4x D5 G83 to Z-0.5 (peck 3).
3. T2 D6: pocket drafted walls semi-finish, z-levels 1 mm, 0.15 left; holes D6.4 helical (r0.2, pitch 1) to Z0.05
   (holder 30 mm stick-out), low centre feed because edge feed = 16x centre feed.
4. T3 D3: opening floor corners at Z15 (contour, ap 2).
5. T4 D1.5: drafted pocket walls finish, z-levels 0.4 mm (cusp 0.035), 3-sided contour into the sharp corners.
6. T6 D6 ball: R3 top round-overs, waterline passes at theta 0..90 step 10 deg, straight passes extended past part.
7. T9 chamfer: countersinks D12x90, 2 circular passes per hole (tip Z28.2, 27.2).
