# Critical review checklist: 3+2 refinement phase

_Fixed 2026-11-20 23:50 UTC, before any result of this phase existed. Do not edit this file after results are seen.
Additions that only tighten go in the dated addendum at the bottom._

## Who reviews, and what they get
- **Fresh agents.** Each reviewer is a fresh agent: not a fork, with no access to the developer's conversation or
  summary.
- **Inputs.** They get only:
  - this file;
  - `docs/ACCEPTANCE_REFINE.md` and `docs/BRIEF_REFINE_3PLUS2.md`;
  - the repository;
  - the run directories;
  - the report pages, which they render themselves.
- **Goal.** The reviewer's job is to **find reasons to fail the work**. A PASS needs evidence: a file:line, a number
  recomputed by the reviewer, or a screenshot path the reviewer took and **opened and looked at**. "Looks fine"
  without evidence is a FAIL.
- **Output.**
  - `docs/reviews/critical_review_refine.md`: one row per item, PASS / FAIL / N/A, with evidence.
  - Defects ranked by severity: blocker, major, minor.
- **Effect of a FAIL.** Any FAIL on an OWNER item, or any blocker, blocks the showcase claim until it is fixed and
  re-reviewed by a fresh reviewer.

Environment: on the owner's machine everything runs in WSL (`wsl.exe -d Ubuntu-26.04`):
- repo: `~/cnc-llm`;
- venv: `~/cnc-llm/.venv/bin/python`;
- `MILLBILL_DIR=~/millbill`;
- Playwright Chromium is installed, with WebGL via SwiftShader (`--use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader
  --enable-unsafe-swiftshader`).

`tools/review_report.py <report/index.html> <outdir>` takes a standard screenshot set and dumps
`window.__machineCheck`, console errors and timings. Use it, **and** take your own screenshots of anything you
doubt. The script is a convenience, not evidence of its own correctness.

## A. Score and evaluator (OWNER W2, W6, W7)
1. **Recompute the headline yourself** from the probe dump (`final/probes.npz`: P, N, area, dev, edge, witness) using
   your own few lines of numpy. It must match `metrics.json` to 0.01 %.
   - The excluded area must be exactly edge band plus tab witness.
   - The edge band must be ≤ 0.9 % and the witness ≤ 1.5 %.
   - No witness probe may have dev < −0.1.
2. **Run the attack tests and add to them.** At minimum, write and run programs that:
   - a. leave the part on big tabs or a full web;
   - b. skip the underside / parting face;
   - c. hide proud material in the edge band (cut everything except a skin along face edges);
   - d. skip a whole side face.

   Each must score the skipped area as failed, or be INVALID. Confirm these exist as tests in `tests/` and pass.
3. **Harness tampering.**
   - Edit one line of `sim5.py` in a scratch copy, run the trusted evaluation, and confirm it says INVALID.
   - Write a generator that imports `cncllm.sim5`, and confirm it says INVALID.
4. **Wording.** `grep -riE "unreach|not reachable|excluded|hand-finish"` over the generated brief, feedback, metrics
   and report text of the final runs.
   - Allowed: "edge band (metrology) excluded", "tab witness excluded", and diagnostic hints that clearly say they
     do not change the score.
   - Any failed area described as unreachable or excluded is a FAIL.
5. **MillBill agreement** in `final/evaluation.json` must be ≥ 99.99 %. Re-run the cross-check on one run yourself.
6. **Clean checkout.** The final evaluation was re-run from a clean checkout of the frozen commit. Check the hashes
   in `evaluation.json` against the addendum of `ACCEPTANCE_REFINE.md`.

## B. Part-off, rigidity, fixture (OWNER W3, W4, W5; PROPOSED W3a, W4a)
1. **Tabs.** Tab count, per-tab section area and smallest width (twice the inscribed radius), and total.
   - Recompute from the final material yourself: section the exact workpiece at the weakest plane and at 0.2 mm
     below the part.
   - Validity: total 2–30 mm² and ≤ 15 % of the section; each tab ≤ 3.0 mm; ≤ 4 tabs.
   - W3a: 2–4 tabs, 20–30 mm².
2. **Witness.** Witness area in mm² and as a % of surface. Target ≤ 0.5 %; above 1.5 % is a FAIL.
3. **Close-up.** Render or screenshot the part-off close-up. Answer as a machinist: can these tabs be snapped or cut
   with hand tools and filed flush, with no saw? Is the underside machined everywhere except the tab witnesses?
4. **Rigidity.** No rigidity error before `(PART-OFF)`. Weakest plane ≥ 30 % after every setup.
5. **Fixture.**
   - There is one `fixture.json`, and no re-clamping.
   - `plan.md` gives reasons for the orientation that follow from the geometry and the `fixture-suggest` output.
   - Say whether you agree.

## C. Report and page quality (OWNER W1, W9 proxy; PROPOSED W13)
Render at 1440×900 and 390×844, in light and dark (`prefers-color-scheme`). **Look at every screenshot.**
1. **Machine view is the default.**
   - In ≥ 3 re-orientations, screenshot before, mid-swing and after.
   - The cradle, platter, vise, stock and part visibly rotate together, and the spindle and tool stay vertical.
   - `window.__machineCheck` reports max spindle-direction error ≤ 1e-6, A/C equal to `sim5.frame_angles` for every
     setup, and max tip mismatch versus Part view ≤ 1e-6 mm.
2. **Machine drawing.**
   - Cradle, trunnion supports, platter, vise, stock, stub, part, spindle nose and holder are drawn, in plausible
     proportions.
   - A DRO shows X Y Z A C, the tool and the current line, and updates during play.
   - Material removal is visible in tilted orientations.
3. **Headline.**
   - The headline number is the whole-surface score.
   - Tab witness and edge band are shown separately and are small.
   - No "of reachable surface" wording.
4. **Final result view.**
   - Every out-of-tolerance region shows as failure (proud or gouge colour). Tab witness has its own colour, with a
     legend.
   - Clicking a region or problem explains why it failed: the nearest passes, and a diagnostic hint.
5. **Part-off section.** Tab count, sizes, witness, and a close-up image.
6. **60-second test.** From the page alone, in 60 seconds, write down:
   - what the part is;
   - the whole-surface score;
   - whether it is valid;
   - the three biggest problems;
   - how the part is held and parted off.

   Compare with `metrics.json`. Any wrong or unanswerable item is a FAIL.
7. **Craft.** Score each of these 1–5 from the screenshots:
   - typography and hierarchy;
   - alignment and spacing;
   - colour use and contrast, light and dark;
   - 3D scene legibility: framing, lighting, can you tell what you are looking at;
   - phone layout: no horizontal scroll, nothing clipped or overlapping;
   - honesty: nothing overstated.

   **Any score below 4 is a FAIL.** For each FAIL, give the screenshot and the fix.
8. **"Best it can be".** List the five changes that would most improve the page for a non-machinist friend of the
   owner. A major one that is cheap to fix and not fixed is a FAIL.
9. **Technical.**
   - Opens from `file://`.
   - 0 console errors.
   - Viewer ready in < 5 s on a laptop GPU; under SwiftShader, report the time and allow up to 15 s.
   - G-code downloadable.

## D. Results quality (OWNER W8 intent, W10; PROPOSED numbers)
1. **FTC-10.** VALID; whole-surface ≥ 95 % (aim 98 %); no gouge region > 1 mm². Report the real number either way.
2. **Every failed region > 2 mm² in `final/regions.json`.** Check its stated cause against the G-code: is there no
   pass, the wrong tool, a stepover too coarse, or a pass that stopped short?
   - Check it against `docs/FTC10_REACH_AUDIT.md`. Any region the audit shows reachable with the frozen library and
     fixture, that the program did not attempt and the handoff does not list, is a FAIL ("silent miss").
3. **CTC-04 ×0.2 held out.**
   - It was run with the frozen skill and harness.
   - The run notes show no developer intervention (git log of the run dir).
   - The result is reported honestly in the handoff.
4. **Visual sanity of the result.** View the final deviation images and the 3D result yourself. Do the claimed
   in-tolerance areas look finished (no stair-steps or unmachined skins)? Does the underside look machined?

## E. Machinist read of the G-code (PROPOSED W12)
1. **Feeds versus chip load**, per tool: compute F / (flutes × S) from the program. Flag > 1.3× the library chip load.
2. **Stick-out and reach.** Flag any tool cutting deeper than 4×D below the holder face without a reason.
3. **Grip and stub.** Grip depth versus roughing loads; chatter risk.
4. **Air cutting.** Share of cutting moves that remove nothing (`vol == 0`). Flag > 40 % of cutting time.
5. **Safe indexing.** Every `G68.2` / `G69` is preceded by M5 and a retract. Spindle restarted before cutting.
6. **Part-off.** One pass per side; smallest section last; tab positions reachable for the operator.
7. **Verdict.** RUN AS IS / RUN WITH MINOR EDITS / DO NOT RUN, with reasons.
